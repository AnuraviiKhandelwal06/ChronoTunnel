PROJECT: MetroPulse
TITLE: Urban Mobility Intelligence Platform

DATA SOURCE:
MTA Subway Hourly Ridership 2020-2024
https://data.ny.gov/Transportation/MTA-Subway-Hourly-Ridership-2020-2024/wujg-7c2s

DATE COVERAGE:
Monthly dataset: January 2020 - December 2024
Hourly sample: 120 selected dates across 60 months
Hourly sampling: One Wednesday and one Saturday per month

DATASETS:

1. Monthly Station-Level Dataset
File: monthly_ridership_station_level_2020_2024.csv
Records: 25,679
Months: 60
Stations: 428
Total Ridership: 4,802,664,920
Total Transfers: 197,901,742

2. Hourly Station-Level Dataset
File: hourly_ridership_sample_station_level_2020_2024.csv
Records: 1,172,916
Selected Dates: 120
Original Records: 1,214,193
Total Ridership: 312,730,792
Total Transfers: 12,367,003

3. Station Coordinate Reference
File: station_coordinates.csv
Station Complexes: 429
Missing Coordinates: 0
Coordinate Method: Ridership-weighted representative coordinates

PROCESSING:
- Monthly data aggregated by month and station complex.
- Hourly data aggregated by timestamp and station complex.
- Coordinate variants consolidated for station-hour records.
- Representative station coordinates maintained separately.
- Original interim datasets preserved.

VALIDATION:
- Monthly ridership and transfer totals preserved.
- Hourly ridership and transfer totals preserved.
- Hourly duplicate station-hour records: 0
- Missing hourly coordinate matches: 0
- Hourly daily files successfully processed: 120

SAMPLING NOTE:
The hourly dataset is a representative sample, not a complete
hourly record of every day from 2020 through 2024.

LIMITATIONS:
The dataset measures ridership and transfers. It does not directly
measure passenger occupancy, train capacity, overcrowding,
train frequency, or complete passenger journeys.
